Connect Real Google Accounts

To enable 1-click official Google login, you need a free Google OAuth Client ID. When users sign in, Google returns their real name, email, and photo, and our app instantly creates their real account in the database!

Quick 2-Minute Setup Steps:

  1. Visit Google Cloud Console Credentials (free).
  2. Click Create Credentials → OAuth client ID.
  3. Choose Web application.
  4. In Authorized JavaScript origins, add: http://localhost:8080 and http://127.0.0.1:8080.
  5. Copy the Client ID and paste it in the box above!
